What Is Ludo Wordo?

Ludo Wordo is an innovative strategy-based board game where players collect alphabets, move pawns intelligently and complete a target word to win the match.

  • Strategy-based gameplay
  • Alphabet collection mechanics
  • Word completion challenges
  • Educational and competitive gameplay

The Core Objective

  • One target word appears at the start of every match
  • Some alphabets of the word are missing
  • Players must collect the missing alphabets
  • The first player to complete the word wins

Core Gameplay

  • Players move pawns based on dice rolls
  • The Ludo path contains alphabets
  • Landing on squares collects alphabets
  • The game continues until a player completes the word

Alphabet Collection

  • Collected alphabets are stored in the Alphabet Collection page
  • The top shows the target word
  • Your collected alphabets appear in the middle
  • Opponents’ alphabets appear below
  • Players can strategise using this information

7 Words a Week – Root Based Learning

  • Every week players learn seven words connected to a root word
  • Day 1 introduces the root word and first word
  • Six more words appear across the week
  • Each word includes meaning, examples and grammar usage
  • Understanding the root helps remember all words easily

Spin Wheel Rewards

  • Spin the wheel when coins run out
  • Earn coins and bonuses
  • Daily login rewards available

Leaderboards

  • Global leaderboards
  • Weekly and monthly rankings
  • Seasonal rewards for top players

Mystery Boxes

  • Unlock surprise rewards
  • Get special cards and bonuses
  • Enhance gameplay strategy

Winning the Game

  • First player completing the word wins
  • Coins and rewards are granted
  • Words are added to House of Words
